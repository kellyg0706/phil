## The actual finding: a directional-bias miss, not routine variance

The pre-R1 rows (9c5be5868fda/c67d8b6559ef, Sep 21) were honest, modest,
correctly-vetoed calls — own 0.47/0.44 beat the market's more extreme
0.385/0.611 on the eventual Yes/No outcome, and the veto (no sourced
poll-to-outcome SD for Brazilian elections) was the right discipline call
either way.

The runoff-day rows (e73da232eca4/4f6b3cd01db9, Oct 25) are the real story.
Final-week valid-vote polls were nearly unanimous for Flávio: Datafolha
47/48, Quaest 45/46, PoderData 48/49, AtlasIntel 48.4/49.9, Futura 47.9/48.2
all had Flávio ahead by 1-2pts; only Vox had Lula up. Aggregators put
Flávio +1.3 to +2. Own model additionally shaded further toward Flávio
citing a "2018/2022 R1-understated-the-right" precedent, landing at
Flávio 0.78/Lula 0.22 — the market agreed almost exactly (0.80/0.20). Lula
won. This was a confident, converging call from five pollsters, the
market, and the agent's own directional prior, and it was wrong. No bet
was placed (market-agrees skip), so there's no P&L damage, but the
calibration miss is real: both forecasts land in the 0.7-0.8/0.2-0.3
bucket with an outcome that bucket should see ~20-30% of the time, not
100% wrong.

**Root cause, not just outcome:** the "R1 polls understate the right"
prior driving the extra shade past the raw poll numbers was itself built
from exactly ONE prior instance — the Oct 16 Rio governor retro
(RETRO-20261016-1700), where PL candidate Ruas beat his polls by sweeping
undecideds. Applying a single down-ballot governor's-race data point as a
standing national-presidential prior was the method error — different
race level, different electorate, different turnout dynamics. **Playbook
edited** (`strategy/playbook.md` "Estimation method" step 1, Brazil 2026
runoffs note): the Rio-governor-based right-lean shade is retired as a
standing prior. Down-ballot and presidential races just broke in opposite
directions on the same hypothesis (n=1 each way) — no basis to keep
leaning either direction without a sourced multi-election base rate. The
2f6a83c8276a total-vote/valid-vote method-error lesson from DEEP-2026-10-05
is unaffected and stays in force.
