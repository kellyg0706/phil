## Estimate, fill or variance?

**Mostly an estimate error.** The 0.87 was a base rate with no same-day
evidence. The note says so: "No Nov 11 coverage found yet, so base-rate
estimate". The base rate came from the Nov 4-10 post-midterm posting
sprees. Three things it missed:
- **Resolver strictness.** Yes needs a qualifying insult: a personal or
  professional disparagement or a derogatory nickname aimed at a real
  person. Policy criticism and plain reposts do not count, and the source is
  "a consensus of credible reporting". A high posting rate is not the same
  as a reported, qualifying insult on one specific ET day.
- **The day itself.** Nov 11 was Veterans Day, a ceremonial day. Memorial
  Day 2026 still had "Dumocrats" posts, so this lowers the rate rather than
  zeroing it. A WebSearch this tick found no coverage of a Nov 11 2026
  insult.
- **The book was already lower.** Its mid of 0.68 priced the doubt, and I
  overrode it with a base rate and no dated evidence. This is the familiar
  pattern of a self-built prior that is more confident than a book I have no
  information edge on.

The only other row of this market type is a6a8177980af (Sep 9, est 0.98,
WON). It had a CONFIRMED same-day reported insult (Haberman, "Maggot")
before recording. The contrast is the lesson: the evidence-backed row was
right and the base-rate-only row was wrong. That is n=2, so this is a
recording rule that only adds restriction, not a calibration claim.
