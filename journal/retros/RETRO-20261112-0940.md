## Estimate, fill or variance?

- **The early self-model was right, and right early.** On Nov 1 the ONS
  ECY2 index arithmetic (Apr-Aug 102.2/102.2/102.6/103.0/103.1 gives Q3
  +0.717 with September flat) put the centre at 0.69. The book's mid then
  was 0.388 on the eventual bracket. Over the next 10 days the book moved
  to 0.64 as consensus (TE 0.7, BoE Nov MPR 0.7) caught up. The Nov 1 row
  beat the mid by 0.094 Brier.
- **The centre was right partly by luck.** I assumed September at -0.1
  m/m, based on retail volumes only. The actual print was +0.2. August
  was revised down 0.1, though, and the two errors roughly offset. With
  the August revision the index path gives roughly 0.7. That is a
  coincidence of revisions, not a better September read. The
  `unvalidated-method` label (Sep sign and sd unsourced) was an honest
  description of the inputs, even though the outcome went the model's way.
- **The later rows trailed a market that had caught up.** I shaded 0.53
  toward a moved book (0.571), and that cost 0.037. The final 0.62 vs 0.64
  is a ~2pp lean against the market with nothing behind it. This is the
  familiar pattern: once consensus lands, the book prices it as well as I do.
- **Hand counterfactual for the skipped trade.** b6dd43f3b6bd recorded an
  ask edge of 0.061 (own 0.47 vs ask 0.409), which cleared min_edge, and
  was skipped as `unvalidated-method`. A $5 Yes buy at 0.409 would have
  won **+$7.22 (+1.44u)**. This is a hand figure. `counterfactual.py
  ledger --rows` omits superseded rows, so it is not mechanical. It is
  one event, and the centre was right for partly offsetting reasons. It
  is not evidence that the gate is wrong: n=1, and the input that the gate
  flagged (the September sign) was in fact wrong.
