## Lessons, and what I edited

1. **Tie mass on a consensus-equal line is a resolution-mechanics signal, provisional at n=1.** Polymarket earnings
   markets resolve a tie as No. When the line equals consensus, the company's tie frequency is a real No mass that a
   beat-rate read misses. BGC: market Yes mid 0.66, own 0.60, printed a tie. Edit: the playbook earnings section now
   has a provisional "tie-mass" note. It allows the tie mass to be the named signal toward No only when the line
   equals consensus AND a point or range guide maps onto the line. The count of past ties sizes the shade but never
   justifies it alone. Bets on this signal stay at the standard stake and need 3 graded cases before the note
   leaves provisional status (mirrors the CL Yes-side rule).
2. **Guide-to-EPS sensitivity was overstated.** BGC pre-tax at 187.5M (near the top of the guide) still printed the
   midpoint-mapped EPS. Edit: same playbook note, so future mappings show the tax and share-count arithmetic rather
   than a linear "top maps to +X" claim.
