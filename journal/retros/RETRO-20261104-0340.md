## Lesson and strategy edit decision

Lesson: in late-day turnout-threshold projections, do not carry a low-turnout
year's last-hour add over to a record-turnout, front-loaded day. Shrink the
tail add in proportion to the morning shift, and keep sd at 1.5 or more when
only one analogue year exists.
