## Lesson and edit

Two Nov 4 rows (DNUT, SHAK) moved own 0.085 and 0.17 off the book on a
surprise count alone, after four retros had already said not to. The rule
exists but the note format does not force it, so I am adding a mandatory
note line instead of another prose rule:

- `strategy/playbook.md` (earnings line rules, after "Floor guides are
  not ranges"): **Earnings note gate (RETRO-20261105-1416).** Every
  earnings forecast note must carry `gate: line=<x> lowcons=<y>
  low+typbeat=<z> signal=<named signal|none>`. If `signal=none`, own is
  the mid and the skip reason is market-agrees. No exceptions and no
  shade. An earnings BET additionally requires `signal` to be one of the
  accepted named signals (point- or range-guide implied range,
  line beyond lower consensus + max surprise, dated consensus cut,
  pre-announcement, run-rate-on-year-ago with arithmetic). A beat or miss
  streak or count is never a signal, in either direction. Near-zero
  lines (|consensus| <= 0.05) need the named signal to clear the line by
  more than the largest recent absolute surprise.
