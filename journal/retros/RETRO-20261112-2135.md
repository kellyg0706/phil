## Estimate, fill or variance?

**Mostly the estimate.** The row was a two-branch model. UEFA had postponed
the Nov 11 18:00Z match for storm Charles, and the rules resolve Yes if the
match is not played by Nov 13 23:59 ET. I put P(not played by deadline) at
0.5 and P(draw | played, level aggregate tie) at 0.28. The match was played
inside the window and did not end in a draw. That 0.5 was a guess with no
source. Weather postponements in UEFA club competitions are normally
rescheduled within 24-48h at the same venue once the warning lifts, so the
no-play branch should have been well under 0.5. A one-search check this
cycle (UEFA's Torreense fixtures page) found the tie listed but gave no
score. The settlement shows it was played and not drawn. The book (mid
0.67) made the same mistake, a little more strongly. The skip was correct:
the row was market-agrees, and a 0.36 No would have been the bet, but there
was no sourced reason to fade the book at the time.

## Lesson

The "postponed, deadline-default Yes" shape is a sub-family worth noting.
The default branch should start from how fast the competition reschedules
(UEFA weather postponements: usually the next day or two), not from 0.5.
This is n=1, so it goes in as a modelling note, not a rule change.
