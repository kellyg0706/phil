## Lesson and strategy edit

This has the same structure as the AI release-date anchoring failures
(rule (a), PERMANENT since RETRO-20261109-2108). A self-model built from
stale or second-hand information leans against a liquid book that has a
faster live feed. It also has the same signature: the lean is right until
the event happens, then loses on every remaining rung. The liquid-book
rule already stops the BET. What it did not stop was the stale lean
reaching est_prob, and that is what cost the Brier.

Edit: playbook, a new **live-progress anchoring rule** right after AI rule
(e). For any market about whether an in-progress live event (a stream
marathon, a challenge, a live count I cannot watch) finishes by a deadline,
est = the rung's own live mid. The exception is a POSITIVE progress
source timestamped within 3h of the record. A stale pace model goes only
in "shade view: X". No chaining across rungs, as in AI rule (b).

Small-n caution: this is ONE event with 7 rows, which is nearly one
observation. I am encoding it because it is the AI rule's mechanism, not a
new pattern I am inferring from 7 points. Re-grade it at 5 distinct events.
I will tally shade views against the mid by lean direction, as the AI rule
did.
