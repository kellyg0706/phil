The playbook's RETRO-20261007-2030 rule is to record the modal leg at or

  It won big and beat the mid (-0.1273). That makes dated OIS/futures

**Verdict:** the rule held for the fourth ladder in a row. Every row that
broke it lost to the mid, and every row that followed it beat the mid.
Keep the rule. Two scoped clarifications go in the playbook. (1) When
bank calls flip after a central-bank statement, the rule applies to the
NEW modal from the first dated flip. Data printed before the flip does
not count as a contrary source. (2) A dated OIS/futures read counts as a
dated contrary source. When the PM book lags it, do not shade below the
OIS number toward a slower venue (an undated Kalshi page) or the economist
consensus. Evidence: this bet, recorded at 0.27 against OIS at 0.32-0.37,
when the result was a hike.
