## Lesson → playbook edit

Added "China CPI YoY ladders: the market's modal bin is the prior
(RETRO-20261109-0420)" at the end of `strategy/playbook.md`.

- It is a recording rule. On a China CPI YoY ladder, the estimate on the
  market's modal bin may sit below the mid by at most 0.05, unless the note
  quotes a dated, sourced consensus that puts the centre in a different
  bracket. Tail brackets inherit the mass that frees up, so they stay thin
  where the market is thin.
- It is a betting rule. A No fade of the market's modal China CPI bin is
  barred. Fades of off-modal brackets stay forecast-only under
  unvalidated-method, as before.
- Re-grade after 3 more prints (Nov, Dec, Jan releases). If the modal bin
  loses 2 or more of those, lift the cap.

This moves my honest belief toward the market because the market has
outperformed my model 3 of 3 on this exact shape. It does not game
calibration.
