## Mexico: right side of the bet, wrong centre

**The bet won for the right reason, but only partly.** The rationale was
"the print is not in 2.0-2.5, since that needs a >1pp miss against IGAE".
That held. The print was 3.2, so the miss against my IGAE-built centre
was 0.4pp. The ledger bet did not need a precise centre, so the win is
real. The fill was fine (spread 0.01). This is a correct estimate on the
side I staked.

**The ladder shape was the estimate failing.** My centre was 3.6 with sd
~0.3, and I put 0.58 on >=3.5 against a 0.09 mid. The market centred on
roughly 3.2-3.3, in line with the older Aug nowcast (3.2) and El
Financiero's pre-release average (~3.4) that I had quoted and then
over-ridden with my own IGAE + IOAE arithmetic. The IGAE-to-timely-GDP
gap was +0.15 in Q2 (IGAE SA avg ~1.95 vs box 2.1) and -0.4 in Q3, so that
mapping is noisier than the 0.3 sd I gave it.

**The veto earned its keep.** The two wide-spread-veto rows are
counterfactuals. On >=3.5, buying Yes at the 0.113 ask would have lost
the whole 5 USD (-1.00u). On 2.5-3.0, No at about 0.90 would have won
about +0.11u. Net, the veto saved about +0.89u on this print. Per the
DEEP-2026-09-30 ruling these are graded here, not appended to the frozen
table. `core/counterfactual.py ledger` reconciles them mechanically.
