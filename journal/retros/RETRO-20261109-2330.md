### What was right and wrong

1. **TTD was an estimate error, not variance.** My named signal was "line beyond
   the guide-implied range". The case ran: the guide implied about 0.21, Q2
   missed its own 260M EBITDA guide by 7%, and reaching 0.27 needed EBITDA
   about 21% over the guide. That guide was a reset. It implied a 34%
   sequential EBITDA drop (241M to 160M) with no stated cause, and the print
   beat it by 66%. A post-miss reset guide is a floor, not a centre. Reading
   the prior miss as evidence against the next quarter got the direction
   wrong. The playbook now says so (see the edit below).
2. **The RKLB and HIMS GAAP guide constructions were right.** On RKLB, the top of
   every range rounded to -0.07, which is not greater than the line, and the
   book at 0.39 was rich. On HIMS, the line needed about 15M over the
   guide-implied breakeven. Both resolved No. GAAP lines have fewer exclusion
   levers than non-GAAP (TTD excluded 44M of restructuring), so the guide
   construction is more trustworthy there.
3. **OXY: under-confident on an 8-for-8 beat streak.** This is small (+0.027
   total), but it repeats a known pattern where my "consensus fell on oil, so
   haircut" read cost a little against books at 0.90 to 0.97. With n=3 rows on
   one event, it does not justify a rule.
4. **Cat in the Hat.** The early Friday-multiplier rows (2.7-3.5x, then 3.4-4.0x)
   centred at 17.5-18.2M. The final was 20.0M, a 4.0x multiplier on 5M Friday
   with previews. So the 23:25Z Nov 7 >=20 row at 0.45 against a mid of 0.705
   was wrong by +0.216. My note there said "own model 0.20 shaded toward book",
   and the book was right. On the Sunday row (688c, 0.84): the >=20 edge needed
   a -2.4% revision. Under the 5% sd rule that is z = -0.49, so P = 0.69, and
   I recorded 0.84 ("unsourced revision sd"). That is above what the rule
   gives, so it is a conformance slip, not a calibration win. The realized
   revision was -2.43%, which left the outcome sitting on the line (20.003M).
