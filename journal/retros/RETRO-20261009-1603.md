## Verdict

- Row 1 was a bad estimate, and the reason is structural: the
  all-windows bootstrap samples 10h windows from every hour of the day,
  most of them covering the account's dead overnight hours. Here the
  remaining window (06-16Z) covered the entire posting band, so
  all-windows overstated P(0 more) by a lot. The 0.50 aligned/recent
  read saw this. The rule from RETRO-20260925-1812 says to record
  all-windows when aligned n<20, and that rule did the damage here.

  high. The mid at 0.73 was nearer.

recording rule, not a betting change): added the tally row and a
**diurnal-band exception** to the record-the-mechanical-read rule.
When a post-count account's last-7-day xtracker hourly posts fall
>=80% inside a band of <=8 UTC hours, the all-windows bootstrap is a
defective input for any remaining window that covers or excludes that
band. In that case, record the hour-aligned read (or the band-rate
Poisson read when aligned n is tiny) and put all-windows in the note as
"all-windows view: X". Re-grade at 3 events.
