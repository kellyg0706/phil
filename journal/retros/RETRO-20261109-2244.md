### What was right and wrong

1. **Basis ambiguity was the whole story (estimate error on 9d44, partly on
   38c2).** On Nov 7 I modelled two regimes: Fri-Sun only, or Wed/Thu folded
   into the weekend. I put P(fold) at about 0.5. The resolver used Fri-Sun and
   left the full Thursday opening day out. With P(fold) near 0.1, the 06:35Z
   8-9 rung would have been about 0.3 rather than 0.15, roughly level with the
   mid. This is a fact about how The Numbers (and so this resolver) treats a
   Thursday opener. It was not variance.
2. **The early ≥9 read beat a 0.87-bid market (6c16).** That row used the same
   two-regime model. Even at P(fold) 0.5 it said ≥9 was unlikely, and the mid
   of 0.91 was badly wrong. The 0.91 book was probably pricing in the fold
   reading. The final landed at 8.94M, only 0.6% under the edge, so the outcome
   was closer than the -0.706 makes it look.
3. **Sunday-estimate rows (5c01/1fd2/c96e) behaved as the 5% sd rule
   predicts.** The 9.0 edge sat at z = +0.81 sd at 5%, so P(8-9) was about
   0.72 and P(≥9) about 0.21, and I recorded 0.72 and 0.20. Both were recorded
   at 16:21Z Nov 8, before RETRO-20261109-1845 added the "rung within 1 sd
   never > 0.70" cap, so 0.72 is not a conformance breach. Under the cap it
   would now be 0.70.
4. **Estimate-to-final revisions on record are now +4%, -3.2% and +3.35% (n=3).**
   There is still no direction, and the +3.35% did not bear out my note that
   Indian distributors revise more than wide releases (it was within the normal
   range). The 5% sd stays.
