missing from the 06:03Z cycle. `core/resolve.py` `settlement_ts()`

rows identical across runners. These four rows say 08:02:02Z, but they
were settled by this cycle's 08:33Z resolve run. So a settled_ts
earlier than the cycle that reports it is expected, not a missed row.
