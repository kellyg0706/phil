### The Fable rows broke the rule, and the breaches were wrong

The playbook's "AI release-date anchoring rule" (RETRO-20260928-2015) says
that with no primary source giving a date, est_prob = ladder mid. A lean goes in the note as
"shade view: X". "Not out yet" earlier on the day moves nothing. All three rows did
what the rule forbids:

- cd72944f6d07 (Nov 8): est 0.50 vs mid 0.675, built from "no official announcement"
  plus leak dating.
- 091c61d2f40d (Nov 9 13:50Z): est 0.08 from "anthropic.com/news shows no post" and a
  "daily hazard from own by-Nov-19 0.50". That is the forbidden "not out yet" input,
  chained off the cd72 breach.
- eb80da59dbd6 (Nov 9 16:18Z): est 0.08 "matches on-Nov-9 sibling". The second breach
  seeded the third.

So one breach became a family. A breached est on one rung was used as the hazard input
for its siblings, and the error compounded across the ladder.
