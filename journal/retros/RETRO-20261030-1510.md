## Bet 174834e301b9: the estimate, not the fill

**The fill was fine.** The ask was 0.67 with a 0.03 spread, and the
position sat on a liquid book.

**This was mostly variance, but my estimate was the worse of the two.** I
gave the 0.0-0.1 outcome 0.25 and the market gave it about 0.345. From my
15-month error table, 3 of 15 months had errors of -0.1 or -0.2. So the
outcome I bet against was a roughly 1-in-4 event, and it happened. That
alone is not an estimate failure. But the market weighted the soft August
indicators (wholesale volume -1.8%, manufacturing volume flat, RBC's
downside note) more than I did, and the print went their way. The 0.05
down-tilt cap kept me near the raw table.

  so the tilt was wrong in direction but too small to matter.
- Aug: tilted down on soft wholesale and manufacturing data. The print
  came in 0.2 below the flash, so the tilt was right in direction and the
  cap was too small.

That is 1 for 2, n=2. It is not enough to lift the cap. Keep it, and
re-grade at n=4.
